Why GenLayer is Central

Traditional prediction markets (Polymarket, Augur) break down when resolving subjective or complex claims: they either rely on centralized oracle committees or slow dispute voting rounds.

  • On-Chain Web Access: Contract fetches external news/data directly via gl.nondet.web.get().
  • LLM Arbiter: GenLayer nodes execute gl.nondet.exec_prompt() with nuanced resolution instructions.
  • Comparative Consensus: GenVM validators verify that the resolution criteria and web evidence logically lead to the verdict.
